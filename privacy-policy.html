Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Anthem Plumbing collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website, phone calls, and service requests.

Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of June 26, 2026. Anthem Plumbing may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide when you call us, submit a form, request service, or communicate with our team.

  • Name, phone number, email address, service address, and appointment details.
  • Information about your plumbing, heating, cooling, or water heater needs.
  • Website usage information such as browser type, pages visited, device data, and referral source.
  • Call, text, email, or form submission records used to schedule and support service requests.

How We Use Information

We use information to respond to requests, schedule appointments, provide estimates, complete service, improve our website, and communicate with customers.

  • To contact you about service requests, estimates, appointments, or follow-up questions.
  • To provide plumbing, heating, cooling, emergency, and related home services.
  • To improve website performance, customer experience, and internal operations.
  • To comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, and protect our business and customers.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, tracking pixels, or similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to improve marketing and performance. You can adjust browser settings to block or delete cookies, but some site features may not work as intended.

Sharing Information

We may share information with trusted service providers, scheduling tools, marketing vendors, payment processors, or business partners who help us operate the website and provide services. Text, Email, and Phone Communications

By submitting your contact information, you authorize Anthem Plumbing to contact you by phone, text, or email about your request. Message and data rates may apply. You may ask us to stop non-essential communications at any time.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. No website or communication method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may request that we update, correct, or delete information you have provided, subject to legal, operational, or recordkeeping requirements. You may also opt out of non-essential marketing communications.
